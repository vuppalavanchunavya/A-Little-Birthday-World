# Product Requirements Document (PRD)
# An Interactive Birthday Surprise Website for My Sister

**Project:** A Little Birthday World  
**Birthday:** 30 October 2026  
**Experience type:** Interactive, personalized birthday website  
**Platform:** Mobile-first responsive web application  
**Primary objective:** Create a delightful, emotional, playful birthday experience inspired by the supplied Instagram Reel.

## 1. Product Vision

Build a personalized birthday website that feels like opening a handmade digital gift. Instead of scrolling through a conventional webpage, the birthday girl should interact with the experience, make choices, unlock surprises, explore memories, and discover a heartfelt letter.

The experience should combine:
- A cute, playful birthday aesthetic.
- A blue-and-white scrapbook and stationery theme.
- Interactive yes/no choices and gift selection.
- A personal photo album and letter.
- Smooth animations and delightful micro-interactions.
- A birthday-specific reveal on 30 October 2026.

The website should feel carefully designed, emotionally meaningful, and polished enough to be shared as a special gift.

## 2. Reference Design Analysis

Use the supplied screen recording as the primary visual reference.

### Visual characteristics to reproduce

- A light cream or white background with small blue polka dots.
- Navy-blue decorative borders, ribbons, bows, stars, and cute illustrations.
- Handwritten-style headings paired with readable body text.
- Illustrated envelopes, gift boxes, gems, cakes, and flowers.
- Scrapbook-style photographs arranged at different angles.
- Interactive choices that lead to different screens.
- A letter displayed alongside a collage of photographs.

Use these characteristics as the design direction, while creating an original, polished website rather than copying the Reel frame for frame.

### Improvements over the reference

The finished website should:
- Fit modern mobile screens without requiring awkward zooming.
- Use larger, more accessible interaction targets.
- Have smoother transitions and consistent spacing.
- Load quickly and avoid unnecessary animation effects.
- Make every gift choice meaningful and functional.
- Use actual photographs and personalized messages rather than generic placeholders.
- Include a coherent beginning, middle, and emotional ending.

## 3. Target User and Context

**Primary user:** My sister, who will receive the birthday website link.

**Sharing method:** WhatsApp, Instagram, or a direct website URL.

**Primary device:** Mobile phone.

**Secondary devices:** Tablet and desktop.

No account, login, registration, or app installation should be required.

## 4. Complete User Experience

### Screen 1 — The Birthday Invitation

Create a charming, full-screen welcome page.

**Visual design**
- Cream background with tiny blue polka dots.
- A centered illustrated birthday card or gift box.
- Small floating stars, bows, and hearts.
- A subtle animated sparkle effect.
- Handwritten-style text.

**Suggested content**

"Hey, someone very special has a little surprise waiting for her… 💙"

Below it, display:

"Are you ready to open your birthday surprise?"

Buttons:
- **YES, SHOW ME! 💝**
- **NOT YET 🙈**

**Interaction**
- Clicking the affirmative option triggers a gentle transition into the next screen.
- Clicking "Not Yet" produces a playful, affectionate response and lets her try again.
- Keep the interaction lighthearted rather than making the user feel blocked or frustrated.
- The invitation should respond smoothly to taps.

### Screen 2 — The Birthday Countdown and Reveal

This screen must respond to the date automatically.

**Before 30 October 2026**
- Display an animated countdown to the birthday.
- Show a cute illustration of a wrapped gift or birthday cake.
- Display: "Something special is waiting for you…"
- Include a button to continue exploring the available surprises.

**On 30 October 2026**
- Replace the countdown with a celebratory reveal.
- Display "IT'S YOUR BIRTHDAY! 🎂"
- Reveal her name with an animated text effect.
- Trigger confetti, floating balloons, and gentle sparkles.
- Play optional birthday music after user interaction.

**After 30 October 2026**
- Show the birthday celebration by default.
- Never show a negative countdown.
- Keep all gifts, photographs, and messages accessible.

Use the `Asia/Kolkata` timezone. The birthday begins at 00:00 on 30 October 2026 in India Standard Time.

The countdown must calculate time dynamically, not rely on hardcoded values.

### Screen 3 — "Accept Your Gifts?"

Create the playful interaction shown in the reference.

**Design**
- A centered blue-and-white illustrated card.
- A cute character or animated birthday illustration.
- The heading: "Accept Your Gifts? 🎁"
- Two large buttons: YES and NO.

**YES interaction**
- Animate the card opening.
- Trigger sparkles and a small burst of confetti.
- Transition to the gift-selection screen.

**NO interaction**
- Show a playful message such as "Hehe, try again! Your surprises are waiting 💙"
- Animate the illustration or gently shake the card.
- Allow another attempt.

The interaction should remain fun and affectionate. Provide accessible buttons and avoid an endless or frustrating interaction loop.

### Screen 4 — Choose Your Gift

Create an interactive gift-selection screen.

Heading: **"Yay! Choose Your Gift!"**

Display three illustrated gift objects in a scrapbook layout.

**Gift 1: The Envelope 💌**

Represents a heartfelt letter from me to my sister.

On clicking:
- Animate the envelope opening.
- Reveal a personal letter.
- Display a handwritten-style heading.
- Allow the user to return to the gift-selection screen.

**Gift 2: The Diamond 💎**

Represents the memories and special moments we share.

On clicking:
- Animate the diamond sparkling.
- Reveal a photo album or scrapbook.
- Show photographs with personalized captions.
- Allow the user to navigate through the memories.

**Gift 3: The Wrapped Present 🎁**

Represents the final surprise.

On clicking:
- Animate the gift box opening.
- Reveal a personalized birthday message, a special photo collage, and the final surprise.
- Trigger a celebratory animation.
- Provide a way to revisit the other gifts.

Each gift must lead to a distinct experience. Do not create decorative buttons that have no functionality.

The user should be able to explore the gifts in any order without losing progress.

## 5. Gift Experience A — The Personal Letter

Create a digital letter inspired by the reference's open-envelope and stationery design.

### Visual design

- Cream stationery with blue decorative details.
- A navy-blue ribbon or envelope seal.
- A handwritten-style title.
- Subtle paper texture or shadow.
- A small decorative illustration in the corner.

### Interaction

1. Display a closed envelope.
2. The user taps the envelope.
3. Animate the envelope opening.
4. Reveal the letter with a smooth text animation.
5. Allow the user to read the complete message without waiting for a slow animation.

### Content

Start with a placeholder:

"To my favorite human,

Happy Birthday! 💙

No matter how much we fight, laugh, annoy each other, or go through together, you'll always be someone incredibly special to me.

I'm so lucky to have you as my sister. I hope this new year of your life brings you happiness, beautiful memories, endless laughter, and everything your heart wishes for.

No website could ever fit all the love I have for you, but I wanted to make this little corner of the internet just for you.

Happy Birthday, my forever person. ❤️"

This message must be editable and replaceable with my own words.

Include a "Back to Your Gifts" control.

## 6. Gift Experience B — Our Memory Scrapbook

Build a visually rich, interactive photo album inspired by the recording.

### Layout

Create a two-page scrapbook layout on larger screens and a comfortable, vertically scrollable layout on mobile.

**Left page**
- A decorative heading such as "Our Little World".
- A collection of photographs arranged like physical prints.
- Blue washi tape, paper clips, small doodles, and hand-drawn stars.
- Captions beneath selected photographs.

**Right page**
- A personal message or short story.
- Additional photographs pinned at different angles.
- Small decorative illustrations and dates, when provided.

### Interaction

- Tap a photo to enlarge it.
- Allow navigation between photographs.
- Provide a close button to return to the scrapbook.
- Add subtle page-turn transitions.
- Allow users to return to the gift-selection screen.

### Photo requirements

Create an easy-to-edit photo configuration.

Each photograph should support:
- Image path.
- Optional caption.
- Optional date.
- Optional short memory description.

Use placeholders during development. Do not invent actual family memories or present stock images as real photographs of us.

The finished scrapbook must be easy to personalize by replacing the images and captions.

## 7. Gift Experience C — The Final Surprise

This should be the most emotionally memorable part of the website.

### Opening animation

- Show a closed illustrated gift box.
- Ask: "Ready for your final surprise?"
- On tapping, animate the lid opening.
- Reveal a photo collage with confetti and glowing stars.

### Final birthday message

Use an editable message such as:

"Happy Birthday to the girl who makes life a little more beautiful just by being in it. 💙

You're not just my sister; you're a part of my world that I will always cherish.

I hope you smile a little more today, feel a little more loved, and remember how special you are—not just on your birthday, but every single day.

This little website is just a small reminder of how much you mean to me.

Love you always. Happy Birthday! 🎂❤️"

### Closing interactions

Include:
- A "Replay My Surprise" button.
- A "Back to Your Gifts" button.
- An optional button to open the photo gallery again.

The ending should feel like the conclusion of a journey rather than another ordinary website section.

## 8. Optional Birthday-Day Extras

Add these features if they improve the overall experience without making the website unnecessarily complicated.

### Birthday music
- Optional background music.
- User-controlled play and pause.
- Start playback only after a user gesture.
- Use an audio file that is licensed or otherwise permitted for use.

### Interactive birthday cake
- Display an animated cake with candles.
- Ask the user to make a wish.
- Let her tap to extinguish the candles.
- Trigger a confetti effect and display a birthday wish.

Microphone access must remain optional.

### Photo montage
- Display selected photographs with gentle transitions.
- Keep controls available to pause or navigate.
- Ensure photographs remain visible long enough to appreciate.

## 9. Date-Based Experience Logic

Use a centralized date configuration.

```ts
export const birthdayConfig = {
  name: "YOUR_SISTER_NAME",
  birthday: "2026-10-30",
  timezone: "Asia/Kolkata",
};
```

Implement the following states:

| Date in India | Experience |
|---|---|
| Before 30 October 2026 | Countdown and accessible preview |
| From 30 October 2026 | Birthday celebration and all gifts unlocked |
| After 30 October 2026 | Full birthday experience remains available |

The date logic must:
- Use the actual current time.
- Respect India Standard Time.
- Update automatically without redeployment.
- Work after page refresh or reopening.
- Avoid negative countdowns.
- Allow the complete experience to be tested using simulated dates during development.

## 10. Design System

### Color palette

- Background: `#FFFDF8`
- Primary navy: `#173B65`
- Light blue: `#D9EAF7`
- Accent blue: `#6CA6D9`
- Soft cream: `#F8F0DF`
- Highlight gold: `#E9C779`
- Body text: `#334155`

Use blue and cream as the dominant colors. Reserve gold and pink for small decorative accents.

### Typography

- Headings: A playful handwritten font such as Caveat.
- Body: A readable font such as Inter or Nunito.
- Personal letters: A restrained handwritten-style font for headings, with readable body text.

### Reusable design elements

Create reusable components for:
- Polka-dot backgrounds.
- Scrapbook paper.
- Illustrated gift cards.
- Buttons and hover/tap states.
- Photo frames.
- Decorative ribbons and stickers.
- Confetti.
- Transition animations.
- Navigation controls.

The visual style must be consistent across all screens.

## 11. Technical Requirements

### Preferred technology stack

- React with Vite.
- TypeScript.
- Tailwind CSS.
- Framer Motion for transitions.
- CSS animations for lightweight decorative effects.
- Lucide React for functional icons, supplemented by original illustrations where appropriate.

Use custom CSS and SVG illustrations for the cake, envelope, gift box, and decorative elements when this creates a more cohesive visual style.

### Suggested structure

```text
src/
  components/
    WelcomeScreen.tsx
    BirthdayCountdown.tsx
    BirthdayReveal.tsx
    GiftAcceptance.tsx
    GiftSelection.tsx
    LetterGift.tsx
    MemoryScrapbook.tsx
    FinalGift.tsx
    BirthdayCake.tsx
    MusicController.tsx
    ConfettiEffect.tsx
    NavigationControls.tsx

  config/
    birthdayConfig.ts
    memories.ts

  assets/
    photos/
    music/
    illustrations/

  utils/
    birthdayDate.ts

  App.tsx
  main.tsx
```

### Architecture

- Use reusable React components.
- Keep birthday content in configuration files.
- Use React state to manage screen transitions and gift interactions.
- Avoid unnecessary backend infrastructure.
- Do not require authentication or a database.
- Ensure every gift can be revisited.
- Keep date logic separate from visual components.
- Use optimized images and lazy loading where appropriate.

## 12. Mobile-First Requirements

The experience must be designed for mobile first.

- Use full-screen or near-full-screen storytelling layouts.
- Keep all interactive targets comfortably tappable.
- Avoid horizontal overflow.
- Make the scrapbook readable without zooming.
- Adapt the two-page album to a vertical layout on smaller screens.
- Keep animations smooth on typical mobile devices.
- Test on common mobile browsers and desktop browsers.

## 13. Accessibility, Privacy, and Performance

- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
- Ensure buttons have accessible labels.
- Maintain adequate contrast and readable font sizes.
- Do not require microphone permissions.
- Do not collect personal information.
- Use photographs only when supplied or authorized for use.
- Avoid unnecessary third-party scripts.
- Ensure the experience remains usable when music or animations are unavailable.
- Provide a graceful fallback for failed image loads.
- Avoid large animation libraries or assets when lightweight alternatives suffice.

## 14. Deployment

Deploy the finished website using Vercel or Netlify.

The final result must have:
- A working public URL.
- All assets included in the deployment.
- No broken images or routes.
- No requirement to run a local server for the recipient.
- No requirement to log in.
- Correct birthday behavior according to the India timezone.

Provide instructions for replacing photographs, editing messages, and changing the birthday configuration.

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

The website is ready when:

1. The welcome invitation works.
2. YES and NO interactions behave as intended.
3. The countdown displays the correct remaining time.
4. Birthday mode activates automatically on 30 October 2026 at midnight IST.
5. The gift-selection screen displays three working gift choices.
6. The envelope reveals the personal letter.
7. The diamond opens the interactive scrapbook.
8. The present opens the final surprise.
9. The user can navigate between gifts and revisit them.
10. The birthday cake interaction works if implemented.
11. The website is mobile-responsive and visually consistent.
12. All personal content is easy to edit.
13. The website remains accessible after the birthday.
14. The deployed URL works without authentication.
15. The complete experience has been tested before and after the birthday date.

## 16. Final Instructions for the AI Developer

Build a complete, polished, production-ready birthday website based on this PRD and the supplied screen recording.

Do not create a generic landing page with a few birthday cards. Build a connected, interactive journey with a clear visual identity, meaningful choices, smooth transitions, personal memories, a heartfelt letter, and a final surprise.

Prioritize:
1. Visual fidelity to the blue-and-white scrapbook aesthetic.
2. Excellent mobile usability.
3. Meaningful interactions and working navigation.
4. Smooth, tasteful animations.
5. Easy customization of personal content.
6. Accurate birthday countdown and automatic date-based behavior.
7. Fast loading and reliable deployment.

Use beautiful placeholder content during development, but keep all names, photographs, captions, and messages easy to replace.

Complete the entire website, test all interactions, verify the date transitions using simulated dates, and provide deployment instructions.

**The final result should feel like a little digital world created with love, exclusively for my sister.**
